# Nicodemus & Cleo — Brand Guide

## Positioning

**One-liner:** Highlander kitten siblings Nicodemus & Cleo — curled ears, bobtails, zoomies and cuddles.

**Unfair advantage:** Rare breed look + named duo + clear personality contrast + growth-from-kitten story.

Not “random cute cats.” Always lead with duo + Highlander traits when possible.

## Cast

### Nicodemus
- Sweet, smart, plays hard
- Gets visibly excited (bird-watching clicks)
- Bonds with toys (e.g. feather on his back)
- Often the initiator in active play

### Cleo
- Slightly slower to ramp up than Nicodemus
- Cuddly; focused player once locked in
- Signature moments: bunny kicks, focused swatting, finger cuddles/sucking
- Hind legs + wagging tail on cat tree when engaged

## Visual signatures to prioritize

1. Curled ears (close-ups, overhead shots)
2. Both cats in frame for duo branding
3. Bobtails when visible
4. Bright natural light, sharp eyes
5. Floor-level / eye-level play angles for Reels

## Voice examples

### Strong
- “Nicodemus spotted the birds and started clicking. Cleo joined the stakeout.”
- “Nicodemus is parked on a step, swatting up. Cleo’s on her hind legs, swatting back.”
- “Cleo takes her time — then locks in.”

### Weak (avoid)
- “OMG cutest kitties ever 😻😻😻”
- “One’s the tiny warlock. One’s the CEO.” (unless user explicitly wants that tone)
- Vague captions with no names or breed
